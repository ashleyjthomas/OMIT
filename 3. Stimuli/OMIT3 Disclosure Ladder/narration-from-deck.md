## Practice — Sam, Quinn (best friend), Rowan (friend)

| slide | narration | on-slide text |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Before we start, let's practice. This is Sam. |  |
| 6 | Sam has two friends. This is Quinn. Quinn is Sam's best friend. And this is Rowan. Rowan is Sam's friend. |  |
| 7 | Look, one of them is holding a ball. Which one is holding a ball?  [PRACTICE: response training; correct = Rowan (friend)] | Which one is holding a ball? |

| 9 | Sam has some cookies. Who do you think Sam would want to share the cookies with? Why?  [PRACTICE / manipulation check: divisible item; no directional prediction] | Who would Sam want to share cookies with? |
